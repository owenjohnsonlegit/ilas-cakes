A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

A little icing
on the details.

Have another question?
Include it in your cake request.

What kinds of cakes can I request?

Wedding cakes, birthday cakes, and other custom celebration cakes. Tell Ila about your occasion and the design you have in mind.

How do I find out about pricing and availability?

Send a request with your date, approximate servings, and ideas. Ila will follow up about availability and pricing for your cake.

Does sending a request book my cake?

No. A request starts the conversation. Your cake is only booked after Ila confirms your order and any required payment or deposit.

What if I have questions about flavors or other details?

Include your questions in the request. Ila can discuss flavors, dietary considerations, timing, and handoff arrangements with you before you confirm.
