Common questions

Have another question? Ask me in your request.

What kinds of cakes can I request?

I make wedding, birthday, and other custom cakes. Tell me what you have in mind.

How do I find out about pricing and availability?

Send me your date, roughly how many people the cake needs to serve, and your ideas. I’ll get back to you about availability and pricing.

Does sending a request book my cake?

No. Your cake is only booked once I’ve confirmed your order with you and any required payment or deposit is arranged.

Can I ask about flavors, allergies, or pickup?

Yes—include those questions in your request. We’ll go over the details before you book.
